```
dark Celtic country trap, sneering satirical villain rap, Scottish accent, smug posh spoken interludes, Scottish fiddle, bagpipe drone, distorted 808 bass, hard trap drums, rapid hi-hats, church organ, gang chant chorus, sweet psalm-like female vocal on the bridge, bleak highland atmosphere, raw unpolished mix
```
