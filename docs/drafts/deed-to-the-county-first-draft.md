# First draft on Suno: Deed to the County

Step-by-step for one generation on free Suno. Suno's screens change often, so if
a button has moved, look for the same words nearby.

## Steps

1. Go to suno.com, sign in, and click **Create**.
2. Switch from **Simple** to **Custom**.
3. In the model picker, choose **v6-mini**.
4. **Lyrics:** paste the lyrics block below.
5. **Styles:** paste the long styles block. If the box cuts it off, use the short one.
6. **Exclude styles** (under More options, if you have it): paste the exclude block.
   Leave vocal gender unset, because the bridge needs a female voice.
7. **Title:** `Deed to the County T4`
8. Click **Create**. You get two versions (a and b) after a minute or two.
9. Listen to both all the way through, using the checklist below.
10. Heart the better one, open its **Share** menu, copy the link, and paste both
    links into the T4 rows of [the log](../experiment-log.md) with scores.

## What to listen for

- Does the **spoken intro** sound smug, or did Suno sing it?
- Can you hear the **banjo** over the 808?
- Is the **bass** dirty and heavy, or clean and polite?
- Does the voice **sneer**, like he's enjoying himself?
- Does the **chorus chant** stick after one listen?
- Does a **female voice** take the bridge, and does it sound sweet against the rest?
- Does the **beat drop to half-time** in verse 3?
- Does it reach the **outro** ("Rent's due Friday"), or stop early?
- Overall: rough and underground, or glossy country pop?

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Lyrics rejected or words muted | Swap "shit" for "money", then try again |
| Song stops before the end | Use **Extend** on the better version from where it stopped |
| Sounds like country pop | Move "distorted 808, hard trap drums" to the start of Styles |
| No banjo | Start Styles with "banjo-driven" |
| No female bridge | Try again; it's a known weak spot, and useful to know |

Change one thing per retry, and log every try.
